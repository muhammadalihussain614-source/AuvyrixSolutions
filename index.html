Portfolio & Case Studies

Examples of AI and web systems designed to create measurable business outcomes.

AI Chatbot

Client: Home Services Brand

Problem: The company was losing leads after business hours because calls and forms were not being answered quickly.

What Auvyrix built: A website chatbot connected to lead capture, FAQs, appointment scheduling, and routing to the right service team.

Result: Increased lead responses by 68% and reduced missed opportunities during nights and weekends.

AI Sales Agent

Client: B2B SaaS Startup

Problem: The sales team spent too much time qualifying cold leads and following up with low-intent prospects.

What Auvyrix built: An AI sales agent that scored inbound interest, sent tailored follow-up sequences, and flagged warm prospects for human follow-up.

Result: Reduced time to first response from 12 hours to under 5 minutes and improved qualified pipeline volume.

AI Assistant

Client: Consulting Practice

Problem: Admin tasks, scheduling, and email coordination were consuming founder time across multiple clients.

What Auvyrix built: A personal AI assistant for calendar flow, meeting prep, email summaries, and task reminders.

Result: Saved 10+ hours per week and lowered missed follow-ups across client engagements.

AI Documentation Manager

Client: Tech Services Company

Problem: Internal SOPs, client onboarding docs, and support guidance were scattered across drives and chats.

What Auvyrix built: A documentation manager that consolidated knowledge, auto-categorized files, and generated fresh SOP updates from new workflows.

Result: Improved onboarding speed, standardized client delivery, and reduced internal rework.

AI Research Manager

Client: Marketing Agency

Problem: The team spent hours gathering competitor data, trends, and topic insights before campaigns could launch.

What Auvyrix built: A research pipeline that scanned target markets, summarized competitor activity, and generated strategy briefings.

Result: Cut research time by nearly 80% and accelerated campaign planning for multiple client accounts.

AI Inventory Manager

Client: Retail/Wholesale Business

Problem: Inventory planning relied on manual spreadsheet updates and often missed reorder timing.

What Auvyrix built: An AI stock dashboard with restock triggers, purchase forecasting, and alerts based on demand trends.

Result: Reduced stockouts, improved purchasing timing, and improved inventory accuracy across product lines.
